# ORCL Research Sources

**Company:** Oracle Corporation (ORCL)\
**Report date:** September 3, 2026

## 1. Oracle Corporation FY2026 Form 10-K

-   **Date:** Filed June 22, 2026
-   **Link:** <https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1341439/000119312526277521/orcl-20260531.htm>
-   **What I used:** Fiscal 2026 revenue, cloud-infrastructure revenue, operating cash flow, capital expenditures, free cash flow, cash, debt, lease liabilities, preferred stock, management's capex outlook, the RPO recognition schedule, stock-based-award dilution, and mandatory-convertible-preferred-stock conversion terms.
-   **Key line / takeaway:** Oracle generated \$18.101 billion of cloud-infrastructure revenue in fiscal 2026 while capital expenditures increased to \$55.663 billion.
-   **Used in report:** Executive View; Sections 1, 2, 3, 5, and Final Call.

## 2. Oracle Corporation FY2025 Form 10-K

-   **Date:** Filed June 18, 2025
-   **Link:** <https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1341439/000095017025087926/orcl-20250531.htm>
-   **What I used:** Prior management expectations for cloud growth and continued investment in Oracle Cloud Infrastructure, plus fiscal 2025 cloud mix and capital spending.
-   **Key line / takeaway:** Management expected cloud revenue to continue increasing and continued to invest in OCI capacity and geographic expansion.
-   **Used in report:** Section 3, "What management said before — and whether it happened."

## 3. Oracle Corporation FY2024 Form 10-K

-   **Date:** Filed June 20, 2024
-   **Link:** <https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1341439/000095017024075605/orcl-20240531.htm>
-   **What I used:** Management's earlier expectation that cloud services would become a larger share of Oracle's business and that data-center spending would continue increasing.
-   **Key line / takeaway:** Cloud services represented 37% of total revenue in fiscal 2024, and management expected the cloud mix and related infrastructure spending to continue increasing.
-   **Used in report:** Section 3, "What management said before — and whether it happened."

## 4. Oracle Investor Relations — Q4 and FY2026 Results

-   **Date:** June 10, 2026
-   **Link:** <https://investor.oracle.com/investor-news/news-details/2026/Oracle-Announces-Record-Q4-and-FY-2026-Results-Driven-by-Cloud-Infrastructure--Cloud-Applications/>
-   **What I used:** Q4 OCI growth, remaining performance obligations, prepaid/customer-supplied GPU disclosure, and Oracle's fiscal 2027 financing expectations.
-   **Key line / takeaway:** Oracle reported Q4 cloud-infrastructure revenue growth of 93% and \$638 billion of remaining performance obligations.
-   **Used in report:** Sections 1, 2, and 5.
